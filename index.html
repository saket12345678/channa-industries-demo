CNC Plasma Cutting

Using advanced technology, we can draw your designs or ours. Our CNC plasma machine handles production cutting on full sheets of mild steel, stainless steel and aluminium, from thin decorative panels to heavy base plates.

Plasma cutting uses a focused, superheated arc to cut almost any shape in plate or sheet metal quickly and accurately. It is an affordable way to produce lotus motifs, jaali panels, name boards, gate inserts and machine parts, one piece or a thousand.
